# 🐾 PawedCat

Audio pun on "PodCast", with a tad of fluffiness.
A lightweight, distraction-free podcast player for Android. Built with a simple philosophy: **just enough**.

- Enough control
- Enough sharing
- Enough discovery
- 0 ads

---

### Features

1. **Audio & Playback**: Play queue, sleep timer, variable playback speed.
2. **Volume Boost**: Per-podcast volume boost (0 to +10 dB) to level out quiet dialogue.
3. **Downloads & Storage**: Atomic offline downloads, auto-cleanup on episode completion, and regex inclusion/exclusion filters.
4. **Subscriptions**: Directory search and OPML import/export open format.
5. **Universal Sharing**: Shares raw audio stream links that play immediately on any device, messaging app, or browser without installing the app.
6. **Hands-free**: Compatible with Google Assistant voice commands:
   - *"Hey Google, play podcast on PawedCat"*
   - *"Hey Google, play [Show Title] on PawedCat"*
   - *"Hey Google, play [Episode Title] on PawedCat"*
7. **Tested**: Unit tests, component tests with Compose UI, and E2E tests with real Android Emulator.

---

> **Note**: This codebase was conceived, specified, and validated entirely through high-level product decisions, grilling sessions, and live testing on a Pixel 8 — without the author reading the underlying Kotlin or Gradle code.
